# [WO-032] Reconcile RayBot observations with canonical Solana executions

<!-- wallet-observer-task:WO-032 -->

**Milestone:** Optional RayBot

**Status:** Planned; enable only after the required RayBot capability passes qualification. Optional to first-live delivery.

**Website first:** Desktop-browser website and supporting services only. Native mobile, installable PWA, device push and mobile acceptance remain deferred. Monitoring is read-only; no execution, signing keys, new chains or subscription upgrades are authorized.

**Assessment:** [RayBot feasibility review](https://github.com/nhattruong0204/wallet-observer/blob/main/docs/raybot-integration-assessment.md).

## Why we need this

The same trade observed by Helius and RayBot must contribute once to the website and alerts, while disagreements remain inspectable.

## What to do

- Attach RayBot evidence to verified canonical executions through the existing revision and outbox model.
- Keep unmatched or conflicting observations visible as source evidence without creating unsupported trades.

## How to implement

1. Match by chain/transaction, watched actor, qualified instruction identity and verified owner/token flows. RayBot’s documented transaction-level envelope lacks instruction indices; fetch qualified chain detail when needed. Never assign index 0 or match only amounts/time/fee payer to satisfy the schema.
2. Separate delivery identity from execution identity and classify each wallet independently. Handle multiple swaps in a signature, incidental watched accounts, multiple destinations, changed payloads and retried deliveries.
3. Use exact raw integer strings plus verified decimals and chain/address identity. Parse numeric enrichment lexemes as Decimal; preserving their text does not repair upstream rounding. Quarantine contradictory raw quantities and do not rebuild raw values from rounded display amounts.
4. Keep RayBot balance deltas, economic trade legs, rent/fees and native SOL/WSOL separate. Failed transactions, ordinary transfers, liquidity and fee claims cannot become successful buys. Missing parser detail remains unknown.
5. Verify success, slot and confirmation via the qualified chain path; failed:false is not finalization. Preserve both source observations on disagreement and append canonical corrections transactionally. RayBot delivery must not advance a Helius safe checkpoint.
6. Use #8’s bounded chain backfill for canonical coverage. Track RayBot filter/receipt gaps separately: recovery of chain transactions does not reconstruct RayBot-only prices, positions or signals. Keep ambiguous events out of position totals, consensus and notifications.
7. Corroboration should add provenance without sending a second trade alert. #14 must key delivery intent to canonical identity and apply correction policy, not provider arrival count.
