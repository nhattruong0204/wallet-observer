## Why we need this

First-live estimates depend on a feed that actually covers the watched wallets, supported swap routes, and reconnect recovery. Writing the UI before proving this can leave a convincing but unusable application.

## What to do

- Evaluate one primary Solana provider and its decoded stream plus historical lookup capabilities.
- Define the supported execution types, coverage limits, rate/credit budget, and recovery strategy before implementing the collector.

## How to implement

1. Start with Helius Parsed Streams and its current official documentation; verify account filtering, supported programs, credentials, and confirmation fields against real payloads. Treat decoded instructions as inputs, not automatic proof of a buy.
2. Use a small user-supplied wallet list. Capture sanitized examples of a buy, sell, ordinary transfer, failed transaction, multi-leg swap, wrapped SOL flow, duplicate delivery, and unsupported route.
3. Prove how to recover events after disconnects. If the stream lacks replay, qualify a history endpoint and a bounded overlap/backfill strategy.
4. Measure delivery delay and credit usage for an explicitly recorded sample interval. Document unavailable FOMO-specific fields separately; do not use Wind endpoints as the backend.
