## Why we need this

Aggregating eligible trades can reduce alert noise and show several watched wallets acting on the same token.

## What to do

- Add one versioned configurable signal engine over the personal watch scope.
- Support direction, window, minimum participants, amount threshold, exclusions, cooldown, and alert expiry.

## How to implement

1. Define net_usd as eligible confirmed buy USD minus sell USD; define gross-directional metrics separately.
2. Count distinct wallets unless verified identity links justify person-level aggregation. Store the participating executions and identity basis.
3. Persist rule versions and signal contributions so late/corrected events can update or invalidate a signal reproducibly.
4. Specify occurrence-time windows, allowed lateness, insufficient-price behavior, cooldown scope, and maximum notification rate.
5. Feed signals through the shared canonical live/history and notification paths. Recheck rules for queued delivery and expire stale trading alerts.

## Definition of done

- [ ] A fixture with repeated buys from one wallet cannot satisfy a multi-wallet threshold by repetition alone.
- [ ] A qualifying fixture triggers one signal with inspectable members and totals.
- [ ] Failed/invalidated executions are excluded and corrections reverse their contributions.
- [ ] Replay rebuilds signal state without an alert storm.
- [ ] Token/account filters do not accidentally hide every account-free aggregate signal.
- [ ] Thresholds are personal settings, not claims of exact Wind algorithm parity.
