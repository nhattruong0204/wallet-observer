# [WO-009] Add token metadata, quote caching, and market-data provenance

<!-- wallet-observer-task:WO-009 -->

**Milestone:** First live

**Plan:** [BUILD_PLAN.md](https://github.com/nhattruong0204/wallet-observer/blob/main/BUILD_PLAN.md)

## Why we need this

Useful trade cards need decimals, names, and available market context, but stale or missing prices must not become fabricated execution values.

## What to do

- Resolve token metadata and cache current quotes for tokens in the watch scope.
- Keep execution valuation separate from current market valuation.

## How to implement

1. Use chain/address keys and timestamped provider adapters. Request only observed or opened tokens, with bounded TTL refresh and concurrency.
2. Retain raw execution quantities; compute historical USD values only from a sufficiently contemporaneous and attributable price source.
3. Expose price source, observation time, stale/missing state, and available market fields. Do not treat missing market cap or liquidity as zero.
4. Reject late stale updates overwriting newer quotes. Handle duplicate symbols, multiple pools, decimal corrections, and token-not-found responses.
5. Batch requests where the provider supports it and surface usage against the configured monthly budget.

- [ ] Exact chain/address lookup cannot mix tokens with the same ticker.
- [ ] Price outages leave raw trade quantities and explorer links usable.
