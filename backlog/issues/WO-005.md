## Why we need this

The collector needs one unambiguous watch scope, and you need to adjust it without editing source code or creating a product account.

## What to do

- Provide add/list/edit/pause/remove operations for wallet watches, notes, aliases, and groups.
- Add a small bulk import/export contract with per-row validation and an explicit initial list of 10–25 wallets.

## How to implement

1. Validate addresses by chain and reject malformed inputs without case-folding Solana addresses.
2. Separate removing a watch from deleting its historical records. Record monitoring states such as resolving, active, paused, unsupported, and error.
3. Make repeated additions idempotent. Preview imports and return valid, duplicate, and invalid rows without silent partial success.
4. Store manual identity labels as manual. Use group membership for filters and later signals; do not call a wallet label a verified person.
