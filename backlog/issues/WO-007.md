## Why we need this

A decoded instruction or incoming transfer does not necessarily mean the watched wallet bought a token. Incorrect attribution would contaminate every downstream view and alert.

## What to do

- Convert supported source records into canonical economic executions and explicit unknown/unsupported events.
- Separate trade execution outcome from chain confirmation and preserve revisions.

## How to implement

1. Attribute balance changes and execution legs to the watched wallet; account for fees, wrapped SOL, token-account creation/rent, and routed swaps.
2. Merge legs only with sufficient transaction/instruction evidence. Keep two independent orders or swaps separate even if time and amount match.
3. Use fixture-driven route handlers for programs proved in WO-002. Unsupported routes remain visible with a reason and source link.
4. Track observed/confirmed/finalized/corrected chain facts without inventing off-chain FOMO order states. Failed transactions cannot contribute to confirmed trade totals.
5. Apply later status changes idempotently; publish revisions and reverse any now-invalid derived contributions.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Representative buy, sell, transfer, failed, routed, and wrapped-SOL fixtures classify correctly.
- [ ] Amounts retain raw precision and fees are distinguished from acquired/sold quantity.
- [ ] Transfer-only records do not trigger buy/sell alerts.
- [ ] Duplicate delivery creates one appropriate execution; distinct swaps are not collapsed.
- [ ] Confirmation/status corrections are reflected in history with provenance.
