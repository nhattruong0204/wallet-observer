## Why we need this

Clan participation is meaningful only with a known membership denominator and historical membership context.

## What to do

- Persist source-backed clan membership snapshots and calculate versioned participation signals.
- Expose monitored-member coverage separately from authoritative clan size.

## How to implement

1. Gate the feature on qualified membership and effective-time data; otherwise use clearly labelled personal watch groups.
2. Choose written participation/window/amount/tier rules and record the membership snapshot used for each calculation.
3. Deduplicate identities with evidence, use eligible executions, and apply the same corrections/cooldowns/expiry mechanisms as other signals.
4. Show the actual participating members, total authoritative members where available, and number of monitored members.
5. Deliver through the existing feed/Telegram path; no independent audio or wake system.

## Definition of done

- [ ] A membership change produces a new effective snapshot rather than silently rewriting old denominators.
- [ ] Monitored subset size is never presented as total clan size.
- [ ] Signal participants and thresholds are inspectable and reproducible.
- [ ] Missing membership data disables the ratio-based signal with a clear reason.
- [ ] Duplicate/corrected trades do not inflate participation.
